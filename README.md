## 🧭 About me

B.Tech graduate building **automated, reliable cloud infrastructure on AWS** — and the **AI agents** that run on top of it.

After graduating I took a planned career break for personal and family reasons. Rather than let that time go idle, I used it to move from an academic background into DevOps through structured self-study and **hands-on practice on live AWS, Azure and GCP accounts** — provisioning real infrastructure, breaking it on purpose, and learning how to bring it back up.

More recently I've extended that foundation into **AI and Agentic AI engineering**: retrieval-augmented pipelines, tool-calling agents, and the container and CI/CD plumbing that puts them into production reliably.
